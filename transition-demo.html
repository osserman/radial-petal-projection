Projection transition demo

Uses d3's own interpolateProjection technique (observablehq.com/@d3/projection-transitions) unmodified: it blends two raw (λ,φ)→[x,y] functions directly. projection.raw() is what makes the polar petal projection a legal input to that - it's re-anchored so raw(0,0) = [0,0], the convention every d3 raw function shares.
